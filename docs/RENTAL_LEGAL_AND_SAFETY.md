# Rental Legal And Safety Baseline

Date: 2026-07-19
Scope: Gearspot beta release baseline for electric bike, SUP and ski rentals.

## Insurance baseline

1. Basic rental insurance covers accidental damage up to a service-defined cap.
2. Intentional misuse, intoxicated use, and use outside agreed area are excluded.
3. Customer is responsible for deductible according to rental agreement.
4. Theft protection requires lock use and incident report within 24 hours.

## Electric bike safety baseline

1. One rider per bike.
2. Helmet strongly recommended for all riders.
3. Follow local traffic laws and bike-lane rules.
4. Do not charge battery with non-approved chargers.

## SUP safety baseline

1. Life vest required for every rider.
2. Maximum operational load: 120 kg per board.
3. No launch during severe weather warnings.
4. Stay within marked rental area and daylight window.

## Ski safety baseline

1. Standard package: skis, boots, poles.
2. Supported size range: EU 36-46.
3. Helmet mandatory for minors and strongly recommended for adults.
4. Equipment must be inspected before first run.

## Operational note

This baseline is used as release readiness policy for beta operations and must be reflected in customer-facing terms before broad public launch.
